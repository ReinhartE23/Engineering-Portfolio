Project Overview

As a part of my high school engineering course, we were challenged for our capstone project to work with the organization May We Help to make a Halloween costume for a child with a disability. Often times children with disabilities are not able to experience the same events as other children, which can lead them to not feel normal. One of those events is Halloween. Often their disability limits the costume they are able to dress up as for Halloween. The purpose of this project was to change that and to help these kids enjoy Halloween like every other child.

My Role

The child that my team and I worked with was wheelchair bound and wanted to be a backhoe, or as he called it: a "digger," for Halloween. We spent much time making the costume so that it fit to his wheelchair and was just as intrciate as any other costume. However, we spent the majority of our time making the actual arm of the backhoe move up and down at the touch of a button using a linear actuator. This made the costume stand out and allowed us to put our engineering skills to use. This project was amazing and seeing the huge smile on the child's face when we were able to attach it to his wheelchair on Halloween night is why I pursue engineering.

Results and Lessons Learned

This project was one of my first engineering projects and it helped me to develop some electrical engineering experience, soldering experience, and experience in the form of collaboration, problem-solving, and empathy in engineering.
