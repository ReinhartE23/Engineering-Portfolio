Project Overview

In the ND Design Build Fly (DBF) club, we design a high-performance RC aircraft that competes in a series of competitions against other universities. This aircraft must carry a payload, complete a specific task, and perform basic flight for a duration of time.

My Role

I was formerly on the structures team of the DBF club. On this team, I mostly worked on CAD to develop prototypes of the structure of the aircraft. Our design was often constrained based on the designs of the other groups of the team, such as the payload team because they needed a certain amount of space in the main structure to hold the payload. This heavily restricted the design of the structre, but it caused us to need to get creative in our designs for the fuselage of the plane. Apart from CAding, I also conducted some tests on the aircraft to make sure it was ready for competition.

Results and Lessons Learned

From this club I gained CAD experience in the form of Fusion as well as testing/simulation experience. I also gained expereince with different machines such as water jet and laser cutters as well as experience working with carbon fiber.
