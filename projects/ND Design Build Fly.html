Project Overview

In the ND Design Build Fly (DBF) club, we design a high-performance RC aircraft that competes in a series of competitions against other universities. This aircraft must carry a payload, complete a specific task, and perform basic flight for a duration of time.

My Role

While I am no longer a part of this club, I was formerly on the structures team of the DBF club. On thsi team, I mostly worked on CAD to develop prototypes of the structure of the aircraft. Our design was often constrained to the other teams such as the payload team because they needed a certain amount fo space to hold the payload, whcih heavily restricted the design of the structre. Apart from CAding, I also conducted some tests on the aircraft to make sure it was ready for competition.

Results and Lessons Learned

From this club I gained much CAd experience as well as testing/simulation experience. I also gained expereince with machine engineering and working with carbon fiber.
