Project Overview

As a part of my high school science fair I chose to create a device that could create energy in a newly sustainable way. I thought of what was constantly around us and immeditaly thought about how I could turn the sound around us into a renewable source of energy. I created a prototype for this device and it was able to light up a small LED diode. While it still needs more work and testing, it was great experience and led me to receive honors in the science fair.

My Role

I built this device by reversing the speaker system. A speaker system, on the basic level, uses electricty to create sound. I just reversed that process in order to use sound to create energy. This required much research, disassembling, re-wiring, connecting it to a capacitor and light, and reassembling. This device took me a while to make and while it still needs more testing and developing, it was a step in the right direction toward sustainability.

Results and Lessons Learned

This project helped me to develop skills in electrical engineering (circuits) as wll as skills such as problem-solving, resilience, and curiosity.
