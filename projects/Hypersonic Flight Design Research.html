Project Overview

The UCAH Hypersonic Flight Design Competition Research Team at Notre Dame is researching a projectile that will fly in a competition against other schools and even countries conducted by the US military. This projectile must be capable of operating at speeds between Mach 5 and Mach 8 at low altitude. This projectile must be unpowered and must be manueverable in severe atmospheric forces.

My Role

While I just recently joined this team and have not gotten a chance to work on the full scope of the project yet, I have mostly been working on simulations in order to determine strength/temperature testing of the projectile. Later, I will be working with the materials team to find a material for the projectile that can withstand the results of my tests. After this, we will be producing the projectile and then testing it in Notre Dame's Mach 6 and maybe even Mach 10 wind tunnels.

Results and Lessons Learned

From this research project I will gain many skills including computational fluid dynamics (CFD), CAD, simulations through Ansys, researching skills, aerosynamics, material knowledge, and win tunnel experience.
